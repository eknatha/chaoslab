    Guardrails
    
  
    
    
    Protected namespaces
    Experiments are refused against kube-system, kube-public, kube-node-lease, cert-manager, istio-system and gatekeeper-system. Whitespace and casing tricks are stripped before the check, and setting the CHAOS_ALLOWED_NAMESPACES repository variable narrows it further to an allowlist.
    
    
    Bounded blast radius
    Pod-level faults hit a random sample capped at the percentage you choose. Node-level faults are pinned by node affinity to the nodes already hosting your target pods, and never to a control-plane node.
    
    
    Abort on breach
    The hypothesis is re-evaluated every ten seconds during a live run. The first breach removes the fault and triggers a full rollback rather than waiting out the duration.
    
    
    Cleanup that survives failure
    Removal runs on success, failure, timeout and cancellation. Network emulation carries a signal trap and a follow-up scrub job, so a node is never left with a stray tc queue.
    
    
    One experiment at a time
    Runs are serialised per namespace by a concurrency group. Overlapping faults produce results nobody can attribute to a cause.
    
    
    Approval before live
    Live runs execute in the chaos-live environment. Add required reviewers to it and every real experiment waits for a human before it starts. This page also asks you to type the namespace before it dispatches anything.
