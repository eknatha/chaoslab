Container kill: chaos experiment

Kills PID 1 inside the container without deleting the pod. The kubelet restarts it in place, so this exercises restart behaviour rather than rescheduling.

What it tests
restartPolicy, liveness probes, crash loop backoff
The hypothesis holds when
The container restarts in place within seconds and the readiness probe keeps traffic away until it is serving.
It breaks when
Repeated kills push the pod into CrashLoopBackOff, and without a readiness probe requests land on a half-started container.
