Mesh latency injection: chaos experiment

A mesh rule (Istio or Linkerd) delays a fifth of requests by a fixed amount. Timeouts and retries decide whether callers fail fast or wait.

What it tests
request timeouts, retries, circuit breaking
The hypothesis holds when
A short timeout with a retry turns a slow request into a slightly slower one.
It breaks when
No timeout: the slow requests simply wait, and the latency percentile moves with them.
