Mesh abort injection: chaos experiment

A mesh rule answers a share of requests with HTTP 503. Retries multiply the chance of a good answer, at the cost of extra load on a service that is already struggling.

What it tests
retry policy, error budgets, graceful degradation
The hypothesis holds when
Two retries turn a 30% failure rate into under 3%, which a modest threshold absorbs.
It breaks when
No retries: every injected failure reaches the user.
