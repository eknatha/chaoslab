Secret rotated under running pods: chaos experiment for Kubernetes

A credential is rotated at the source. Pods that read the old value at start keep presenting it until something restarts them.

What it tests
hot reload of credentials, restart on change
The hypothesis holds when
Pods reload the secret on change, or a rollout restarts them in step with the rotation.
It breaks when
The old credential is rejected, and the errors continue until someone notices and restarts the deployment.
