Pod kill: chaos experiment for Kubernetes

Evicts a random sample of matching pods. If a PodDisruptionBudget would drop the workload below its floor, the eviction is refused — which is a pass, not a failure.

What it tests
ReplicaSet self-healing, PodDisruptionBudgets, startup time
The hypothesis holds when
Replacements are scheduled and ready before the next request notices, or a PodDisruptionBudget refuses the eviction.
It breaks when
Too few replicas, no readiness probe, or no budget: the survivors carry the load and errors spike during startup.
