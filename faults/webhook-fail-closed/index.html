Admission webhook fails closed: chaos experiment

The policy webhook is down while every pod is recycled, for example during a node pool upgrade. With failurePolicy Fail nothing can be admitted, so nothing comes back.

What it tests
failurePolicy, webhook availability, blast radius of policy engines
The hypothesis holds when
The policy runs fail-open (Ignore) or in audit mode, so an outage of the engine does not become an outage of the workload.
It breaks when
No replacement pod can be created until the webhook returns.
