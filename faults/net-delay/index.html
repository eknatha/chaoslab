Network delay: chaos experiment for Kubernetes

Adds latency on the nodes hosting the target pods only. Slow responses are worse than none: they hold connections open until something upstream gives up.

What it tests
client timeouts, circuit breakers, readiness probe timeouts
The hypothesis holds when
Timeouts shorter than the latency objective fail fast and retries find a healthy path.
It breaks when
Long client timeouts hold connections open and the delay propagates all the way upstream.
