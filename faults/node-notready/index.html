Node NotReady: chaos experiment

The busiest node stops reporting. Its pods drop out of the Service at once, but Kubernetes waits out tolerationSeconds (five minutes by default) before it evicts and replaces them.

What it tests
taint-based eviction, tolerationSeconds, replica headroom
The hypothesis holds when
A short tolerationSeconds replaces the pods quickly, and spare replicas carry the traffic meanwhile.
It breaks when
The default five-minute toleration leaves the workload short-handed for the whole experiment.
