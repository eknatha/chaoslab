Work queue backlog: chaos experiment

Messages arrive faster than consumers drain them and the backlog grows. Scaling on CPU reacts late, if at all.

What it tests
consumer scaling on lag, buffering
The hypothesis holds when
Consumers scale on queue depth and the buffer absorbs the burst.
It breaks when
Backlog turns into latency, then into timeouts.
