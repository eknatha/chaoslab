Zone outage: chaos experiment

Takes every node in one availability zone out at once. Pods concentrated in a single zone disappear together.

What it tests
topology spread constraints, multi-zone capacity
The hypothesis holds when
Replicas are spread across zones and the survivors have the capacity to absorb the lost share.
It breaks when
The scheduler stacked replicas in one zone, so losing it loses them all together.
