Broker partition loss: chaos experiment

A broker leaves and a share of publishes fail until partition leadership moves. Producers that cannot buffer turn that into errors.

What it tests
producer buffering, retries, replication factor
The hypothesis holds when
Producers buffer and retry, and the user-facing path does not wait for the publish.
It breaks when
Every failed publish is a failed request.
