Disk fill: chaos experiment for Kubernetes

Fills ephemeral storage on a node. Once the kubelet crosses its eviction threshold it starts evicting pods by QoS class, lowest first.

What it tests
ephemeral storage limits, eviction thresholds, write paths
The hypothesis holds when
Pods evicted from the full node are rescheduled elsewhere and priority protects the important ones.
It breaks when
No headroom elsewhere, or no priority: pods are evicted at random and the replacements land on the same full node.
