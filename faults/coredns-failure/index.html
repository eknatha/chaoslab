CoreDNS failure: chaos experiment for Kubernetes

The cluster DNS service goes away for every pod, not just a sample. A node-local cache or kept-alive connections are what stand between a DNS outage and an application outage.

What it tests
NodeLocal DNSCache, client-side caching, connection keep-alive
The hypothesis holds when
NodeLocal DNSCache and pooled connections keep most lookups from ever leaving the node.
It breaks when
Every pod needs a fresh lookup at some point, and every one of them fails together.
