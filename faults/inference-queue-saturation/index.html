Inference queue saturation: chaos experiment

Requests arrive faster than the model servers can batch and answer them. The queue grows, then every request waits.

What it tests
event-driven scaling, load shedding, queue buffering
The hypothesis holds when
Scaling reacts on queue depth, and a buffer or shedding policy keeps latency bounded.
It breaks when
CPU-based scaling reacts to the wrong signal, and the queue just grows.
