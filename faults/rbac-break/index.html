RBAC change breaks the autoscaler: chaos experiment for Kubernetes

A tightened Role removes the scale permission from the autoscaler during a traffic rise. The controllers keep running and keep logging, and nothing scales.

What it tests
headroom when scaling cannot help, permission change review
The hypothesis holds when
Standing headroom and a queue buffer carry the rise until the permission is restored.
It breaks when
The surge meets a fixed number of replicas.
