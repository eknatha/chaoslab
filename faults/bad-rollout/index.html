Bad release: chaos experiment for Kubernetes

A new version whose startup is broken is released. A readiness probe stops the rollout early; without one the rollout replaces healthy pods with broken ones. Choose a delivery strategy in the workload panel and watch what changes.

What it tests
readiness gating, rollout strategy, automated rollback
The hypothesis holds when
Readiness gates the rollout, or an automated canary analysis removes the release before it matters.
It breaks when
No probe and a plain rolling update: healthy pods are replaced one by one with broken ones.
