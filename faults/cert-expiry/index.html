Certificate expiry: chaos experiment

The TLS certificate on the edge expires. Every handshake fails, and the pods behind it are perfectly healthy.

What it tests
automated renewal, expiry alerting
The hypothesis holds when
Renewal is automated well before expiry and nobody notices the date.
It breaks when
A calendar reminder was the only control, and it was missed.
